### A1 资源：用简单句安排一天

- 资源类型：`EXPRESSION_LESSON`（表达训练）
- 表达层级：`SENTENCE`（句子）
- 标题：用时间、地点和动作写出一天的安排
- 摘要：用主语、一般现在时、时间和地点信息写出清楚的日常计划，并用 and 连接相关动作。
- CEFR：`A1`
- 最少词数：`30`
- 最多词数：`60`
- 预计分钟：`15`
- 排序：`10`
- 标签：主题选“社会”；文体选“应用文本”；能力选“句子理解、观点组织”；功能选“描述”

正文：

```markdown
## 用时间、地点和动作写出一天的安排

## 1. 一个清楚句子的四个部分

描述日常安排时，可以先回答四个问题：

1. **Who?** 谁做这件事？
2. **What?** 做什么？
3. **When?** 什么时候？
4. **Where?** 在哪里？

基本结构是：

`主语 + 动作 + 时间 + 地点`

- `I read at ten o'clock in the library.`
- `My sister studies English after dinner at home.`

时间和地点的位置可以变化，但初学阶段保持一种稳定结构更容易检查。

## 2. 使用一般现在时

固定安排和习惯通常使用一般现在时：

- `I start work at nine.`
- `He walks to the library.`

当主语是 `he`、`she` 或单数人名时，注意动词的第三人称单数形式：

- `She reads in the afternoon.`
- `Tom goes home at five.`

## 3. 用 and 连接相关动作

`and` 可以连接两个属于同一阶段的动作：

- `I choose a book and sit by the window.`
- `We have lunch and talk about our plans.`

不要在一个句子里堆放太多动作。一个句子表达一到两个紧密相关的动作即可。

## 4. 范例

`On Saturday, I go to the community library at ten o'clock. I return two books and choose a new one. At noon, I have lunch in the small café. I go home at three and read with my sister.`

这段文字按时间顺序展开，每句话都有明确动作。它不需要复杂词汇，也能让读者知道一天如何安排。

## 5. 自检

- 每个句子是否有主语和动词？
- 时间是否前后一致？
- `he`、`she` 后的动词是否需要 `-s`？
- 地点前是否正确使用 `at`、`in` 或 `to`？
- 全文是否按时间顺序排列？
```

- 摘要：通过“原有问题—具体改变—观察结果—下一步计划”组织一篇真实、可验证的数字习惯反思。

- 标签：主题选“健康、科技”；文体选“说明文”；能力选“结构分析、衔接连贯”；功能选“因果、总结”

正文：

```markdown
## 从自动刷手机到有意识使用：一周反思

## 范文

For a long time, I checked my phone whenever I had a free minute. The habit seemed harmless, but it often broke my concentration. I also stayed online too late and felt tired the next morning.

Last week, I tried three small changes. First, I turned off notifications from shopping and video apps. Second, I left my phone in another room while I studied. Finally, I chose two times to read messages instead of checking them all day.

The changes did not make every problem disappear. On the first two days, I still reached for my phone without thinking. However, by the end of the week, I could study for longer periods, and I went to bed earlier on four nights. I also learned that some notifications were useful, so I kept messages from my family and school.

Next week, I will continue the same routine and record when I lose focus. My goal is not to avoid technology. It is to use it for a clear purpose.

## 结构分析

1. 第一段说明旧习惯及影响。
2. 第二段用 `First`、`Second`、`Finally` 列出改变。
3. 第三段同时写困难和观察结果，避免夸大效果。
4. 结尾提出具体下一步，并说明核心态度。

## 可复用表达

- `The habit seemed ..., but ...`
- `I tried three small changes.`
- `The changes did not make every problem disappear.`
- `By the end of the week, ...`
- `My goal is not to ... It is to ...`

## 学习提示

反思文应写自己能观察到的行为，而不是编造精确的健康结论。使用 `often`、`on four nights`、`for longer periods` 等适度表达，比没有依据地声称“效率提高了百分之多少”更可信。
```

- 摘要：学习以问题、原则、具体措施和预期影响组织团队建议，并区分事实、判断与行动项。
- CEFR：`B2`

- 排序：`40`
- 标签：主题选“社会、经济”；文体选“说明文”；能力选“结构分析、观点组织”；功能选“说明、比较、因果”

正文：

```markdown
## 如何写一份可执行的远程协作建议

## 建议文的任务

一份团队建议不能只说“沟通需要改善”。它应帮助读者回答四个问题：发生了什么、为什么值得处理、具体改变什么、如何判断改变是否有效。

## 推荐结构

### 1. 描述问题，不指责个人

写可观察的现象：

`Important decisions are sometimes made in private messages, so other team members cannot find the reason later.`

避免把推测写成事实：

`People do not care about documentation.`

### 2. 提出原则

原则解释建议的方向，例如：`Decisions should be easy to find, while urgent questions should still receive a quick response.`

### 3. 给出两到三项措施

每项措施都应包含负责人、使用场景或完成条件：

- Record final decisions in the shared project page.
- Add an owner and a deadline to every action item.
- Use a short call only when written discussion cannot resolve the issue.

### 4. 说明取舍

新流程会带来成本。写出限制可以提升可信度：

`This rule adds a few minutes of documentation, but it reduces repeated questions later.`

### 5. 提出检验方式

不要预先宣称建议一定成功。可以写：`After two weeks, the team can review whether decisions are easier to find and adjust the rule.`

## 常用衔接

- 提出原因：`because`, `since`, `one reason is that`
- 比较方案：`while`, `whereas`, `in contrast`
- 让步：`although`, `even if`, `this may require`
- 给出结果：`therefore`, `as a result`, `this would allow`

## 修改清单

- 问题是否可以被观察，而不是对人的评价？
- 每项建议是否能由具体的人执行？
- 是否说明了成本或限制？
- 结尾是否给出复盘时间和判断标准？
```

### C1 资源：平衡论证的衔接表达

- 资源类型：`EXPRESSION_LESSON`（表达训练）
- 表达层级：`COHESION`（衔接）
- 标题：平衡论证：连接收益、边界与条件
- 摘要：使用让步、限定、因果和回指建立连贯论证，避免把 AI 教育议题写成简单的支持或反对。
- CEFR：`C1`
- 最少词数：`300`
- 最多词数：`500`
- 预计分钟：`35`
- 排序：`50`
- 标签：主题选“科技、社会”；文体选“议论文”；能力选“逻辑判断、衔接连贯”；功能选“论证、反驳、转折”

正文：

```markdown
## 平衡论证：连接收益、边界与条件

## 1. 平衡不等于各写一半

平衡论证的核心不是机械地列出两个观点，而是说明：一项主张在什么条件下成立，它的限制是什么，以及这些限制如何影响最终判断。

弱论证：

`AI is useful, but it is also dangerous.`

更清楚的论证：

`Although AI can provide rapid formative feedback, that benefit depends on students receiving opportunities to question the feedback and on teachers remaining responsible for consequential assessment.`

第二句使用 `although` 承认收益，再用 `depends on` 指明条件，最终界定责任。

## 2. 四类连接关系

### 让步

- `Although X, Y ...`
- `While this approach may ..., it does not ...`
- `Admittedly, ...; nevertheless, ...`

让步应准确承认对方最强的合理部分，而不是先制造一个容易反驳的弱观点。

### 限定

- `This claim applies primarily when ...`
- `The benefit is limited to ...`
- `Only if ... can ...`

限定词帮助作者避免 `always`、`never` 等过度概括。

### 因果

- `Because the system cannot observe ..., it may ...`
- `This, in turn, can lead to ...`
- `The immediate effect is ..., whereas the longer-term risk is ...`

不要把时间上的先后自动写成因果；应解释中间机制。

### 回指

- `This distinction ...`
- `Such a policy ...`
- `The former ...; the latter ...`

回指对象必须清楚。如果前一句有多个可能对象，就重复关键名词，而不是只写 `this`。

## 3. 段落骨架

1. 主题句提出有限定的主张。
2. 解释主张成立的原因或机制。
3. 给出具体情境。
4. 承认限制或反方理由。
5. 回到主张并调整其边界。

## 4. 修改练习

原句：`AI feedback is fast. Teachers are important. Schools should use AI carefully.`

修改：`AI feedback can be delivered quickly; however, speed alone does not establish whether the feedback is accurate or educationally appropriate. Teachers therefore remain responsible for interpreting it and deciding when it should influence a student's work.`

修改时检查每个连接词是否表达了真实关系。如果删掉连接词后逻辑仍然不清楚，问题通常不在词汇，而在论点本身。
```

- 表达层级：留空

- 摘要：以公共资助排序为情境，区分设计、采购、使用与监督责任，并论证责任不能被“算法建议”所取代。

- 排序：`60`
- 标签：主题选“公共政策、价值讨论、科技”；文体选“学术文本”；能力选“隐含推断、风格控制”；功能选“论证、反驳、总结”

正文：

```markdown
## When an Algorithm Shapes a Public Choice, Who Is Responsible?

Public institutions increasingly use automated systems to rank applications, flag risks, and allocate attention. These systems may help officials process large volumes of information consistently. Yet the language of efficiency can obscure a prior question: when an algorithm materially shapes a public choice, who remains answerable for the result?

One tempting answer is to assign responsibility to the system's developer. Developers do make consequential choices about training data, target variables, error thresholds, and testing. They should therefore document foreseeable limitations and correct defects within their control. Nevertheless, development is only one stage. A technically sound model can still be unsuitable for a particular legal or social setting, especially when the institution applies it to a population or purpose that was not represented during evaluation.

The purchasing institution has a different responsibility. Procurement teams must examine not merely whether a product functions, but whether its decision role is legitimate. A contract cannot transfer a public body's duty to explain why one applicant was favored over another. If officials cannot describe the factors that affected a ranking, they cannot provide meaningful review, even if the vendor regards the model as proprietary.

Front-line users also exercise judgment. Treating an algorithmic recommendation as compulsory may appear neutral, but it is itself an institutional choice. Users need training to recognize uncertainty, record departures, and escalate cases in which the system lacks relevant context. At the same time, it would be unfair to place the entire burden on individual employees if management has created targets that discourage scrutiny.

Responsibility should therefore be distributed without becoming diluted. Developers should be accountable for technical claims and disclosed limitations; institutions for lawful purpose, procurement, and appeal; managers for operating procedures; and authorized officials for final decisions. Independent oversight can test whether these duties are actually performed. A named decision owner, a documented reason, and an accessible route of appeal are practical safeguards because they connect abstract accountability to actions that can be examined.

Some argue that human review is sufficient. However, a nominal reviewer who lacks time, authority, or relevant information may merely approve the machine's output. The important distinction is not between a human decision and an automated one, but between a decision that can be questioned and one protected by procedural opacity.

Algorithms do not erase responsibility. They rearrange the points at which responsibility must be exercised. Public institutions should map those points before deployment, disclose the system's role, monitor unequal effects, and retain the capacity to suspend its use. The question is not whether one actor can be blamed for everything. It is whether every consequential choice has an identifiable owner and every affected person has a credible means of challenge.

## 范文分析

- 开头承认效率收益，同时提出责任问题。
- 中间分别分析开发者、采购机构、使用者和管理者的责任，避免单一归因。
- 反方段落区分“名义上的人工复核”和“有能力的实质复核”。
- 结尾把抽象伦理转化为责任人、理由记录、申诉和暂停机制。
```

## 三、最后为每个任务添加 1 道写作练习

入口：写作任务列表 → 找到对应任务 → “练习”。每题分值建议均为 `1`，先保存再发布。

### A1 · SENTENCE_REWRITE

- 题干：把下面的词组成一个语序正确的完整句子：`at ten o'clock / I / in the library / read`
- 题型：`SENTENCE_REWRITE`
- 配置 JSON：

```json
{ "answer": "I read at ten o'clock in the library." }
```

- 中文解析：一般现在时基本语序为主语 + 动词，随后补充时间和地点。句首大写，句末使用句号。

### A2 · SENTENCE_COMBINE

- 题干：用 `and` 合并句子：`I would like to know the ticket price. I would like to know whether I need to book a seat.`
- 题型：`SENTENCE_COMBINE`
- 配置 JSON：

```json
{ "answer": "I would like to know the ticket price and whether I need to book a seat." }
```

- 中文解析：两个宾语都由 `I would like to know` 引出，可保留共同部分，用 `and` 连接名词短语与 whether 从句。

### B1 · COHESION_FILL

- 题干：填入最合适的连接表达：`I still reached for my phone without thinking. ___, by the end of the week, I could study for longer periods.`
- 题型：`COHESION_FILL`
- 配置 JSON：

```json
{ "answers": ["However", "Nevertheless"] }
```

- 中文解析：前句说明困难，后句转向积极变化，需要转折连接；句首应大写。

### B2 · STYLE_ANALYSIS

- 题干：把带有指责的句子改为可观察、适合团队建议的表达：`People do not care about documentation.`
- 题型：`STYLE_ANALYSIS`
- 配置 JSON：

```json
{ "answer": "Important decisions are sometimes made in private messages, so other team members cannot find the reasons later." }
```

- 中文解析：修改后的句子描述信息存放位置及后果，不推断同事态度，更便于据此设计流程。

### C1 · COHESION_FILL

- 题干：填入表达让步关系的词：`___ AI can provide rapid feedback, teachers must remain responsible for consequential assessment.`
- 题型：`COHESION_FILL`
- 配置 JSON：

```json
{ "answers": ["Although", "While"] }
```

- 中文解析：从句承认即时反馈的收益，主句提出责任边界，Although 和 While 都能构成合适的让步关系。

### C2 · STYLE_ANALYSIS

- 题干：将绝对化表达改写为更严谨的学术句子：`Human review always makes algorithmic decisions accountable.`
- 题型：`STYLE_ANALYSIS`
- 配置 JSON：

```json
{ "answer": "Human review can strengthen accountability only when the reviewer has sufficient information, time, and authority to question the algorithmic recommendation." }
```

- 中文解析：改写使用 `can` 和 `only when` 限定结论，并明确有效复核所需的三个条件，避免无依据的绝对判断。

## 四、发布与前台验收

1. 六个资源全部发布，其中 A2 必须保留有效模板结构 JSON。
2. 新建 A2、B1、C2 任务时，在下拉框中选择本页指定且已经发布的资源。
3. 六个任务分别确认字数范围、预计时间、量表和清单后发布。
4. 每个任务添加并发布一道练习，共 6 道。
5. 前台写作中心按 CEFR 逐级筛选，确认每级至少出现一个资源和一个任务。
6. 打开 A2 任务，确认模板结构可以使用；打开 B1、C2，确认范文引用存在。
7. 用普通学习账号完成一次写作：输入正文 → 对照清单修改 → 保存或提交 → 回到学习记录确认结果。

常见错误：

- `Only templates may contain templateSchemaJson`：非模板资源误填了模板结构；清空该字段。
- `Templates require a schema`：模板结构为空，复制本页 A2 JSON。
- `Rubric total cannot exceed 100`：评分项总分超过 100；本页所有量表均恰好为 100。
- `Invalid checklist item`：清单存在空字符串或重复文本；重新完整复制对应 JSON。
- 引用资源无法选择：资源尚未发布，或资源类型不是 `TEMPLATE` / `MODEL_ESSAY`。
- 发布时提示缺少标签：资源至少选择一个“主题”或“文体”标签。
